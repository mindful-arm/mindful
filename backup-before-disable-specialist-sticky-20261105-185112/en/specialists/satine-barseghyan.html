About the specialist

An important area of Satine Barseghyan’s professional work is eating disorders, with particular attention to eating behavior in children and adolescents. She considers a child’s difficulties in the context of the family environment and, when appropriate, involves parents in the therapeutic process. Her professional interests also include childhood trauma, emotional difficulties, and their impact on a child’s behavior and development.

Main areas of work

Eating disorders, depression, anxiety disorders, obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), intrapersonal difficulties, interpersonal difficulties.

Psychotherapeutic approaches

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT).
