About the specialist

Lena Manukyan’s professional interests include work with adult ADHD and psychosomatic disorders. In clinical practice, she emphasizes an integrated understanding of emotional, cognitive, and bodily experience, helping clients understand how psychological factors affect everyday life and well-being. Ongoing training in trauma therapy has developed her professional skills for working with traumatic experiences and their long-term consequences.

Main areas of work

Depression, anxiety disorders, obsessive-compulsive disorder, psychosomatic disorders, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), complex PTSD, psychological trauma, interpersonal difficulties, intrapersonal difficulties, emotional burnout, attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), loss, grief.

Psychotherapeutic approaches

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Existential Therapy.
