About the specialist

Astghik Mnatsakanyan’s professional interests include ADHD and neurodiversity, with attention to each person’s individual cognitive, emotional, and behavioral profile. She also gives an important place to the relationship between art and mental health, viewing creative experience as an opportunity for self-understanding, emotional expression, and making sense of inner experience. Her academic and research work complements her psychotherapeutic practice and supports a multifaceted view of professional questions.

Main areas of work

Depression, anxiety disorders, obsessive-compulsive disorder, psychosomatic disorders, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), psychological trauma, interpersonal difficulties, intrapersonal difficulties, emotional burnout, attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), postpartum depression, postpartum anxiety, adjustment disorders, neurodiversity, loss, grief.

Psychotherapeutic approaches

Existential Therapy, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Brainspotting.
