About the specialist

In her professional work, Tatev Khachatryan emphasizes the inseparable connection between mental and physical processes, viewing the person as a biological, psychological and social whole. In clinical practice, she seeks to understand not only an individual symptom, but the complete picture of the pathology, and not only the pathology, but the patient as a whole. She values professional responsibility, continuous development and an individualized approach to every clinical case.
