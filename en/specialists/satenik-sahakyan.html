About the specialist

Satenik Sahakyan’s professional work centers on sexual health and on understanding the medical and psychological aspects of related difficulties. She emphasizes safe, non-judgmental communication about sexuality while also taking into account the characteristics of a couple’s relationship. Her professional approach combines evidence-based knowledge about sexual health with individualized clinical work.

Main areas of work

Sexual disorders, vaginismus, painful sexual intercourse, fear of sexual intercourse, reduced sexual desire, orgasm disorders, premarital counseling, erectile dysfunction, ejaculation disorders (premature, delayed).
