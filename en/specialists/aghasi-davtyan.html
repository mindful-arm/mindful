About the specialist

Aghasi Davtyan’s professional work focuses primarily on chemical and behavioral addictions. His training in addiction medicine and psychiatry allows him to view addictive behavior in the context of accompanying mental disorders and to consider their mutual influence and relevance to treatment. He emphasizes patient engagement, continuity of treatment, and relapse prevention.

Main areas of work

Chemical addictions (alcohol, drugs, medications and other psychoactive substances) and non-chemical addictions (gambling), depression, anxiety disorders, OCD, PTSD.

Psychotherapeutic approaches

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT).
