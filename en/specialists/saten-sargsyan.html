About the specialist

In psychotherapy, Saten Sargsyan emphasizes the relationship between a person’s experiences and changing life circumstances, paying attention not only to the difficulty itself, but also to the meaning a person gives to their experience. Her clinical work in oncology has shaped particular experience in supporting people and their families in the context of serious illness, uncertainty and life limitations. In the therapeutic process, she values understanding each person’s individual experience, developing the ability to adapt to change and restoring psychological stability.
