About the specialist

Eduard Aghabalyan’s professional approach has been strongly shaped by his experience as an anesthesiologist-resuscitation specialist working with patients in severe and life-threatening conditions. This experience contributed to his skills in assessing acute and crisis states and to an integrated understanding of physical and mental health. In psychiatric work, he emphasizes comprehensive assessment of the clinical picture, taking into account both mental-health and general medical factors.

Main areas of work

Depression, anxiety disorders, obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), somatopsychic and psychosomatic disorders, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), complex PTSD, personality disorders, eating disorders, psychotic disorders, adjustment disorders, bipolar affective disorder.
