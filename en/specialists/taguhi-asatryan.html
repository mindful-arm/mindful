About the specialist

Taguhi Asatryan’s professional interests center on human relationships—both with oneself and with others. She works with internal conflicts, difficulties in relationships, boundaries and responsibility, as well as emotional burnout. A distinct area of her work is psychological support during difficult life stages, including change, loss and grief, helping people reinterpret their experience and restore internal balance.
