About the specialist

In her professional work, Svetlana Nikoghosyan emphasizes evidence-based understanding of psychological difficulties and the purposeful application of psychotherapeutic methods. She continues her education in clinical psychology in Germany, where she is also becoming familiar with different professional approaches and practices. She is also interested in the relationship between art and psychology, viewing creative expression as a way to recognize and express emotional experiences.

Main areas of work

Depression, anxiety disorders, somatopsychic disorders, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), complex PTSD, interpersonal difficulties, intrapersonal difficulties, adjustment disorders, loss, grief.

Psychotherapeutic approaches

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT).
