About the specialist

Anna Tonikyan’s professional approach combines neonatology experience with specialized knowledge in breastfeeding. In work with mothers and newborns, she emphasizes accurate assessment of the medical and physiological causes of breastfeeding difficulties while taking into account the specifics of the postpartum period. Her professional work aims to support natural and safe breastfeeding in a way that fits the needs of each mother-baby pair.

Main areas of work

Breastfeeding, preparation for breastfeeding during pregnancy, correct latch in the first days of life, lactostasis and mastitis, nipple injuries and cracks, low milk supply and relactation, induction of lactation, false refusal of the breast and bottle, ending breastfeeding.
