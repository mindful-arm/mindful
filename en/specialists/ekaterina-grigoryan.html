About the specialist

Ekaterina Grigoryan’s professional interests include couple relationships and the difficulties that may arise within them. She works with both individuals and couples, addressing conflicts, emotional experiences, and mutual expectations in relationships. In her work, she emphasizes the ability to recognize one’s own emotions and needs, understand personal boundaries, and become aware of one’s role within relationships.

Main areas of work

Depression, obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), anxiety disorders, emotional burnout, interpersonal difficulties, intrapersonal difficulties.

Psychotherapeutic approaches

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT).
