About the specialist

Nana Pambukhchyan’s professional interests include ADHD and neurodiversity, with attention to each person’s individual cognitive, emotional, and behavioral profile. In her work, she also gives an important place to the relationship between art and mental health, viewing creative experience as an opportunity for self-understanding, emotional expression, and making sense of inner experience. Her academic and research activity complements her psychotherapeutic work and contributes to a multifaceted understanding of professional issues.
