About the specialist

Mariam Tonyan’s professional work has a particular focus on psychological trauma and its consequences. Experience gained in different social and clinical settings has deepened her understanding of crisis situations and difficulties in interpersonal relationships. Alongside individual psychotherapy with adults, she is interested in facilitating Balint groups, which focus on the relationship between professionals and patients, their emotional responses, and challenges in professional practice.

Main areas of work

Depression, anxiety disorders, psychosomatic disorders, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), complex PTSD, psychological trauma, interpersonal and intrapersonal difficulties, emotional burnout, adjustment disorders, loss, grief.

Psychotherapeutic approaches

Brainspotting, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT).
