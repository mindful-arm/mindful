About the specialist

In her professional work, Hermine Hakobyan emphasizes a comprehensive understanding of psychological difficulties, considering them in the context of life history, relationships and current circumstances. Around 18 years of work in psychiatric services has contributed to extensive clinical experience with different mental health conditions, as well as skills in professional observation and psychological assessment. She values professional responsibility, an individualized approach and helping people identify their internal resources.
