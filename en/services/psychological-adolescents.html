Psychological Services for Adolescents

Psychological services for adolescents focus on emotional, behavioral, and interpersonal difficulties associated with this developmental stage. They address self-esteem, identity development, emotional regulation, and relationships with family members and peers. The approach considers each adolescent's developmental needs and may involve parents when appropriate.
