Psychological Services for Adults

Psychological services for adults focus on understanding and addressing emotional, behavioral, and interpersonal difficulties, enhancing self-awareness, and improving psychological well-being. Depending on individual needs and therapeutic goals, services are provided in individual, couples, or group formats.

On this page

Individual Therapy Couples Therapy Group Therapy

Individual Therapy

Individual therapy provides an opportunity to explore personal experiences, patterns of thinking and behavior, and factors contributing to the development and persistence of psychological difficulties. It also supports the development of emotional regulation and coping skills.

Couples Therapy

Couples therapy focuses on understanding relationship difficulties, improving communication, and strengthening mutual understanding. It addresses recurring conflicts, trust, emotional intimacy, and personal boundaries.

Group Therapy

Group therapy provides a professionally facilitated setting for exploring personal experiences and interpersonal patterns through interactions with other group members. It supports self-awareness, communication skills, and the development of effective ways to cope with emotional difficulties.
