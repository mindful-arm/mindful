About the specialist

In her professional work, Aneta Melkonyan emphasizes the combination of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic approaches, viewing each clinical case within the full context of the person’s life history and current situation. One of her particular professional interests is perinatal mental health, including support for women’s mental well-being during pregnancy, childbirth and the postpartum period. In expert work, she follows the principles of comprehensive analysis of clinical data and professionally grounded assessment.
