About the specialist

In his professional work, Armen Harutyunyan emphasizes the interconnection between mental and physical processes, viewing each difficulty in the context of the person’s life and individual experience. He combines psychiatric and psychotherapeutic work, focusing not only on reducing symptoms, but also on understanding the psychological processes underlying them. He continuously develops his professional knowledge and psychotherapeutic skills, particularly for work with psychosomatic disorders.
